# Project Summary

## Research question
What exactly does gold protect investors from, and does silver provide comparable safe-haven protection across equities, bonds, crises, and regions?

## Scope
The project uses weekly observations spanning roughly the mid-1990s through early 2025, subject to regional data availability.

Regions:
- United States
- Europe
- United Kingdom
- Japan

Crisis windows:
- Dot-com bust
- Global Financial Crisis
- European sovereign-debt crisis
- COVID-19 crisis

## Analytical components
- normalized price comparisons;
- annual equity returns;
- drawdown analysis;
- rolling volatility ratios;
- rolling beta/correlation analysis;
- crisis/control comparisons;
- static minimum-variance and tangency portfolios;
- 52-week rolling minimum-variance portfolios;
- 8-week smoothing of rolling weights;
- crisis-specific portfolio weights;
- regional portfolio optimization;
- regional frontier comparisons;
- dynamic-versus-static comparisons.

## Reported conclusion
Gold behaves as a state-dependent safe haven that is particularly valuable during severe equity-driven risk-off episodes. Silver is more volatile, more cyclical, and less consistently selected in defensive portfolio allocations.
